The Science Behind Ancestral Valorization

Modern nutrition science confirms what Indian grandmothers knew centuries ago: vegetable skins contain up to 65% of total dietary polyphenols and insoluble fiber, cauliflower stalks hold more calcium and vitamin C than florets, and paneer whey is a complete electrolyte-protein beverage. By valorizing these byproducts, we nourish our families while defending nature.
