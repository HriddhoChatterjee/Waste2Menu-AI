The Science Behind Ancestral Valorization

Modern nutritional biochemistry confirms what Indian grandmothers understood instinctively: vegetable peelings contain up to 65% of total dietary polyphenols and insoluble roughage, cauliflower stems hold more calcium than florets, and paneer whey is a complete electrolyte-protein beverage. By valorizing these byproducts, we nourish our families while defending nature.
